41: ```
42: 
43: ## Signal Components
44: 
45: ### Cortisol (`c`)
46: 
47: Cortisol follows a **dawn-peak** pattern, driven by the hypothalamic-pituitary-adrenal (HPA) axis. In the model, cortisol is sensitive to light exposure and peaks shortly after waking.
48: 
49: $$
50: c(t) \propto E\left[(L - 0.88)^3\right] \cdot E\left[0.05 \cdot (8 - t + d)\right] \cdot E\left[2 \cdot (-m)^3\right]
51: $$
52: 
53: ### Melatonin (`m`)
54: 
55: Melatonin is the **darkness hormone**, inversely related to light exposure. It governs sleep-wake transitions:
56: 
57: $$
58: m(t) = (1 - L)^3 \cdot \cos^2\left(\frac{-(t - 3 - d) \cdot \pi}{24}\right)
59: $$
60: 
61: ### Adiponectin (`a`)
62: 
63: Adiponectin modulates insulin sensitivity and interacts with both cortisol and melatonin:
64: 
65: $$
66: a(t) = \frac{E\left[(-c \cdot m)^3\right] + e^{-0.025(t - 13 - d)^2} \cdot L(0.7(27 - t + d))}{2}
67: $$
68: 
69: ### Glucose (`G`)
70: 
71: Post-prandial glucose is computed as a vectorized function of insulin effect, meal times, and response kinetics:
72: 
73: $$
74: G(t) = \sum_{i=1}^{n_{\text{meals}}} g_i(t, I_E, t_{M_i}, \tau_g, B, C_m, t_{\text{off}})
75: $$
76: 
77: ## Example: Running the Model
78: 
79: ```python
80: from pfun_cma_model import CMASleepWakeModel
81: 
82: # Default parameters — healthy individual at baseline
83: cma = CMASleepWakeModel(N=288)
84:

88: ```
89: 
90: | Signal | Description | Key Property |
91: |--------|-------------|--------------|
92: | `c` | Cortisol | Dawn-peaking stress hormone |
93: | `m` | Melatonin | Darkness-activated sleep signal |
94: | `a` | Adiponectin | Insulin sensitivity modulator |
95: | `I_S` | Insulin Sensitivity | `1.0 - 0.23c - 0.97m` |
96: | `I_E` | Insulin Effect | `a × I_S` |
97: | `G` | Glucose | Summed post-prandial response |
